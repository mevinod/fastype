Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 21, 2026

What data we collect

  • Name: The display name you enter to appear on the leaderboard.
  • Typing results: WPM, accuracy, and score timestamp.
  • Technical session data: Cookie identifier and IP address for 24-hour return detection.

Why we collect it

  • To identify returning users within 24 hours.
  • To show the top 20 leaderboard for the last 24 hours.
  • To prevent duplicate or broken session behavior.

How long we keep it

  • Leaderboard results are kept for up to 24 hours, then removed.
  • Session matching data is used for active session handling and periodically overwritten/expired.

How we keep it safe

  • Only limited gameplay-related data is stored.
  • Cookies are set as HTTP-only to reduce client-side script access.
  • The service is deployed behind server controls (nginx + EC2 access restrictions).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal identifiers such as email, phone, or payment details.
  • We do not use your data for advertising profiles.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site owner via the main website contact details.
